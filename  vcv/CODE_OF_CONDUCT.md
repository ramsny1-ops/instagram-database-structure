# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct to foster an open
and welcoming environment. By participating, you agree to abide by this code.

Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communications.
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback.
- Assume good faith: people make mistakes; be kind when pointing them out.

Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hateful or abusive language, and personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, or other protected characteristics.
- Doxxing, threats, or the disclosure of private information.

Reporting

- If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or email the
  maintainers at: ramsny1-ops@users.noreply.github.com.

Enforcement

- Project maintainers may remove or block contributors who violate the code.
- Repeat or severe violations may result in exclusion from the project.
